# Context and Problem Statement**

In the construction sector, quantity surveying and cost estimation are essential for project planning and management. However, these tasks are often time-consuming, tedious, and prone to manual errors.
Proposed Solution
We propose a mobile application that enables civil engineering professionals and students to perform these calculations directly on their phones. The tool will be fast, reliable, and accessible, while integrating modern features for project management and export.

# **Objectives**

- 	Simplify technical work on-site.
- 	Automate calculations (volumes, reinforcements, blocks, concrete, formwork, etc.).
- 	 enerate a complete cost estimate.
- 	 port results in PDF format.
- 	 Allow users to create and manage multiple projects through personal accounts.
  # **Key Features**
-  Site layout: calculation of stakes, strings, nails, and other setup materials.
-   Excavation and foundation: calculation of excavation volumes, debris, rock, concrete, and trucks.
-   Reinforcement: determination of bars, frames, and tie wires for footings, columns, and beams.
-   Concrete and mortar: calculation of volumes, cement bags, sand, gravel, and water according to mix ratios (Q200, Q350).
-   Blocks: estimation of block quantities and associated mortar.
-   Formwork: calculation of planks, nails, and surfaces to be shuttered.
-   Costs: detailed cost estimate with unit and total prices.
-   Export: generation of a complete PDF project document.
-   Profile: user account management and project history.

# **Technologies**

- 	Target Audience

# **Project Vision**
Our ambition is to make Métrés & Devis a universal tool, useful in Haiti and beyond. The application will combine technical rigor, mobile accessibility, and modernity. It will evolve to include advanced features such as site monitoring, inventory management, and real-time collaboration.
